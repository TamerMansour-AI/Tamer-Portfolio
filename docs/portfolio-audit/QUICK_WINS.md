# Quick Wins

These are recommendations only; none were implemented.

## Five fastest wins

### 1. Replace the 17.28 MB autoplay hero with a poster-first, user-initiated film

Compress the 7.3 MB poster, generate WebP/AVIF, and load an optimized 2–4 MB short film only after intent. Add pause and reduced-motion behavior.

**Why first:** This directly addresses the 23–25 MB homepage transfer and 39.6 s simulated mobile LCP.  
**Effort:** Small–Medium.  
**Impact:** Very high.

### 2. Cut the homepage to three flagship cases

Remove Additional Evidence and Pinterest from the homepage, combine “lanes” with “What I Build,” and link to the archive.

**Why first:** The current homepage has 1,341 words, 26 articles, 64 links, and is 17,047 px tall on mobile.  
**Effort:** Small content/layout edit after approval.  
**Impact:** Very high.

### 3. Add role + outcome to every visible flagship card

Use a compact pattern: “Role,” “For,” “Result,” and one proof link. Remove secondary tags that do not help a buyer decide.

**Why first:** It converts interesting artifacts into professional credibility.  
**Effort:** Small, if evidence is available.  
**Impact:** High.

### 4. Turn Contact into a short route-aware inquiry

Add project type, audience, outcome, timing, preferred language, and contact details; keep email and WhatsApp. State expected response time.

**Why first:** Repeated CTAs currently land on a directory with no qualification or confirmation.  
**Effort:** Medium because an endpoint/privacy decision is required.  
**Impact:** High.

### 5. Fix locale metadata, favicon, and duplicate English routes

Add a favicon; include `/ar/proof/` in alternates; choose root-English or `/en/` consistently; derive navigation and hreflang from one map.

**Why first:** It removes the production console 404 and prevents search/language drift.  
**Effort:** Small–Medium.  
**Impact:** Medium.

## Next five high-priority improvements

6. Replace the 8 MB text-heavy service poster with accessible HTML plus an optimized portrait/artwork crop.
7. Add two permission-safe testimonials or outcome statements with names/roles/organizations where allowed.
8. Move Pinterest, small apps, games, and tooling experiments into a dated Lab with maturity labels.
9. Convert project data to a validated Astro content collection with localized fields and generated routes.
10. Add CI checks for build, links, favicon/console errors, page-weight budgets, and key browser journeys.

## Do not call these “quick wins”

- Replatforming to React/Next.js.
- Adding a CMS before the content model is approved.
- Building elaborate WebGL or scroll-jacking interactions.
- Producing a longer showreel before media budgets and hierarchy are fixed.
- Adding more projects to solve a proof problem.
