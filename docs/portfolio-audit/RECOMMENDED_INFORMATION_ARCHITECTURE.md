# Recommended Information Architecture

## Positioning spine

**Short version:** Palestinian AI creative director, educator, and builder turning stories and complex knowledge into visual experiences, learning systems, and public products.

**Three practice lanes:**

1. **Stories & Media** — cinematic AI films, literary adaptations, music/video, campaign creative.
2. **Learning & Workshops** — training, labs, source-grounded learning, facilitation systems.
3. **Products & Platforms** — archives, bilingual web experiences, educational products, practical AI systems.

“Palestinian / bilingual / culturally grounded” is the lens across the practice, not a fourth miscellaneous category.

Remove Proof as a primary destination. Proof should be attached to every case. Keep a private or secondary evidence index if it helps proposals.

## Homepage

1. **Identity / promise** — one sentence, Ramallah/remote context, primary CTA, selected-work CTA.
2. **Credibility strip** — 3–5 verified facts or partner/audience signals, not vague metrics.
3. **Three practice lanes** — one sentence and one representative artifact each.
4. **Three flagship cases** — Museum of Echoes; AI Verification/Birzeit learning system; Literary Reimaginings or Anbiya production case. Each shows role and outcome.
5. **Ways to work together** — Commission a story, Book a workshop, Shape a product/system.
6. **Selected outcomes / testimonials** — only attributed and permission-safe evidence.
7. **About fragment** — authored Palestinian perspective and working method.
8. **Inquiry decision** — short route-aware brief plus email/WhatsApp fallback.

Target: 600–800 words, 3 flagship cases, one supporting archive link, and less than half the current mobile page height.

## Work

Default view: six selected cases, ordered by strategic value rather than chronology.

Filters:

- Stories & Media
- Learning & Workshops
- Products & Platforms
- Cultural / Palestinian work
- Arabic / English / Bilingual

Secondary “All work / Lab” view may contain experiments, apps, games, Pinterest research, and smaller artifacts. Clearly label maturity: Case study, Released product, Live prototype, Experiment, Archive.

## Case-study template

1. One-line outcome.
2. Hero artifact with accessible poster/playback.
3. Facts: year, audience/client type, role, collaborators, language, status.
4. Challenge or premise.
5. Tamer’s exact responsibility.
6. Approach and key decisions.
7. Process artifacts: storyboards, prompts, source packs, diagrams, screens, workshop activities.
8. Deliverables.
9. Outcome and evidence: release, reach, participation, adoption, feedback, public link.
10. What remained private or cannot be claimed.
11. Related service and one CTA.

## Ways to Work

Use three commercially legible offers rather than an exhaustive capability list:

### Commission a Story or Visual World

For campaigns, cultural stories, music, educational narratives, and short films. Show common starting scopes and what a first sprint produces.

### Book a Workshop or Learning Lab

For organizations, universities, media teams, educators, and communities. Show session formats, languages, outcomes, required setup, and post-session assets.

### Shape a Product or Creative System

For archives, bilingual sites, learning products, prototypes, and AI-assisted workflows. Show discovery sprint, prototype, and systemization paths.

Consulting is the method inside these offers, not a fourth vague service.

## Workshops

- Workshop promise and audiences.
- Three signature formats.
- Named delivered examples.
- Learning outcomes and artifacts participants keep.
- Facilitation languages and remote/in-person availability.
- Practical requirements, typical duration bands, and customization.
- Workshop inquiry form.

## Products & systems

This can live within Work initially and become its own page when there are at least three mature products. Show status, intended user, live/demo link, business model if public, and Tamer’s role.

## Lab

Purpose: protect the flagship portfolio from looking scattered while preserving experimentation.

Include:

- Experimental visual studies.
- Apps and games without full case-study evidence.
- Pinterest direction boards.
- NotebookLM and workflow experiments.
- Early prototypes.

Every item gets a status, date, short learning, and optional public link. The Lab should not compete with flagship work on the homepage.

## About

- Clear biography and Ramallah grounding.
- The through-line from story to teaching to systems.
- Working principles and collaboration style.
- Selected timeline with verified milestones.
- Portraits and behind-the-scenes/process imagery.
- Languages, availability, and safe professional credentials.
- CTA matched to collaboration, not only “read more.”

## Contact and lead flow

Primary form fields:

- What do you want to create? Story/media, workshop, product/system, collaboration, not sure.
- Organization / individual.
- Audience.
- Desired outcome.
- Timing.
- Budget band (optional but useful).
- Preferred language.
- Email and optional WhatsApp.

Show expected response time, privacy note, and what happens next. Keep mailto and WhatsApp. Track only privacy-conscious conversion events.

## Arabic and English structure

Choose one consistent model:

- Recommended: `/en/` and `/ar/` for every localized route, with `/` redirecting or selecting a default; or
- Keep English at root and Arabic at `/ar/`, but remove all duplicate `/en/*` routes.

Generate language alternates and switch targets from one locale registry. If a translation is unavailable, say “English only” and keep the visitor on the equivalent content rather than silently sending them home.

Arabic is not a mirror-only layout. Tune type sizes, line length, heading breaks, image order, and cultural context separately.

### Consolidate

- Merge homepage “lanes,” “What I Build,” “Additional Evidence,” and “Services” into practice lanes + ways to work.
- Merge Proof into case-study evidence.
- Combine TPT and book-to-deck under Knowledge Products where appropriate.

### Reorganize

- Move Pinterest boards to Lab / process research.
- Move NotebookLM from a standalone tool-labeled page into Learning Systems or Lab unless it has a buyer-facing outcome.
- Group apps/games by maturity and relevance.

### Archive or de-emphasize

- Experiments with no clear outcome, role, or current public link.
- Flappy Flip and other casual experiments from the main selected-work grid unless they support a specific product-building offer.
- Duplicate `/en/*` routes.

### Remove from public presentation when needed

- Any private client names, internal process, unapproved metrics, or sensitive source material.
- Placeholder-like cards with no image, link, or evidence from flagship surfaces.
