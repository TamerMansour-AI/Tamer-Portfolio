# Accessibility QA

## Automated result

- Final mobile Lighthouse accessibility: 100.
- Best practices: 100.
- SEO: 100.

## Verified

- English LTR and Arabic RTL landmarks and headings.
- Skip link and visible focus.
- Mobile menu dynamic label, focus containment, Escape close, and focus return.
- `aria-current` on primary navigation.
- Reduced-motion CSS.
- User-initiated controlled video with no autoplay.
- Informative alt text and explicit image dimensions.
- Form labels, required fields, useful native errors, consent, confirmation status, and route preselection.
- Direct email and WhatsApp remain available.
- Contrast corrected on dark project panels, olive evidence band, and footer.
- Internal-link and console-error checks passed locally.

## Viewports reviewed

- 360 px mobile.
- 768/1024 px responsive rules through layout/browser checks.
- 1440 px desktop.
- English and Arabic home, flagship, and inquiry surfaces.

## Limits

This is not a formal WCAG certification. No multi-screen-reader lab was available. The identity film has no speech in the shipped excerpt; if a future version adds meaningful speech, captions/transcript become release requirements.
